## Submitting Expenses

Submit expense claims in **SpendWise** within **30 days** of the expense date, with an itemized receipt for every item above ₹ 500.
Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Approval Limits

| Claim amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹ 25,000 | Line manager |
| ₹ 25,001 to ₹ 1,00,000 | Department head |
| Above ₹ 1,00,000 | Finance Manager |

## What Is Covered

- Client meals, with attendees listed on the claim.
- Work-from-home internet up to ₹ 1,000 per month.
- Approved training courses and certifications.

## Not Covered

Personal entertainment, fines, alcohol without a client present, and upgrades to premium travel classes.

## Payment

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

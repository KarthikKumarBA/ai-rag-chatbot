## Booking

Book all flights and hotels through the company travel desk at least **14 days** before travel when possible.
Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

## Daily Allowance

| Trip type | Meals per day | Hotel cap per night |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic (India) | ₹ 1,500 | ₹ 7,000 |
| International | USD 60 | USD 180 |

## Approvals

All travel needs manager approval in SpendWise before booking. International travel also needs approval from the department head.

## Safety

Register every international trip with the travel desk so we can reach you in an emergency.

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Cloud (a fictional company used for this demo).

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive **24 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month.
Up to **10 unused days** can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive **12 days** of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave cannot be carried over.

## Parental Leave

| Caregiver | Paid leave | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary caregiver | 26 weeks | Can start up to 4 weeks before the due date |
| Secondary caregiver | 4 weeks | Must be taken within 6 months of birth or adoption |

## How to Apply

Request all leave in the **PeopleHub** portal.
Leave longer than 3 days must be requested at least **2 weeks in advance** and approved by your manager.
For emergency leave, inform your manager on the same day and record it in PeopleHub within 2 working days.

## Public Holidays

Employees get 12 public holidays per year. The list for the next year is published in PeopleHub every December.

Welcome to Northwind! Use this checklist during your first month.

## Before Day 1

- Sign the offer letter and the confidentiality agreement in PeopleHub.
- Upload identity and bank documents in PeopleHub (never send them by email or chat).
- IT ships your laptop to your address 3 working days before your start date.

## Week 1

- Complete the **security awareness training** within **7 days** of joining.
- Set up multi-factor authentication on your company account on Day 1.
- Meet your onboarding buddy; your manager assigns one before you join.
- Join the #general, #announcements and your team channels.

## First 30 Days

- Agree on 30-60-90 day goals with your manager by the end of week 2.
- Finish the mandatory courses: Code of Conduct, Data Privacy and Anti-Harassment.
- Book a 1:1 with your HR Business Partner to review benefits.

## Probation

The probation period is **3 months**. Your manager holds a probation review in the last 2 weeks of probation.

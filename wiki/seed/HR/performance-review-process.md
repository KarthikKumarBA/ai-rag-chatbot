## Review Cycles

Northwind runs two review cycles per year: **April** (annual review) and **October** (mid-year check-in).
Promotions and salary revisions are decided only in the April cycle.

## Steps

1. Self-assessment in PeopleHub (1 week).
2. Peer feedback from 3 to 5 colleagues chosen with your manager.
3. Manager assessment and rating.
4. Calibration across the department.
5. 1:1 review conversation and goal setting for the next cycle.

## Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Exceeds | Consistently goes beyond the expectations of the role |
| Meets | Fully delivers the expectations of the role |
| Developing | Partially meets expectations; a development plan is agreed |

## Promotions

Managers nominate employees for promotion during calibration. Nominations need a written case with examples from the last 12 months.
Salary ranges per level are confidential and managed by HR.

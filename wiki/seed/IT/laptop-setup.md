## First Login

1. Power on the laptop and connect to Wi-Fi.
2. Sign in with your company email; you will be asked to set up MFA.
3. Wait for the device management agent to install the standard apps (about 20 minutes).

## Standard Software

Browser, password manager, chat, video calls, VPN client and the endpoint security agent are installed automatically.
Developers can request extra tools in the IT portal under "Software Request".

## Requirements

Do not disable the endpoint security agent or full-disk encryption. Keep the operating system updated within 7 days of a release.

## Getting Help

Raise a ticket in the IT portal or ask in the #it-helpdesk channel. The help desk is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 19:00 IST.

## When You Need the VPN

The VPN is required for internal tools (Grafana, OpenSearch, the admin portals) when you are outside the office network.

## Setup

1. Open the **NorthGate VPN** client (installed on every laptop).
2. Choose the gateway closest to you: Mumbai or Singapore.
3. Sign in with SSO and approve the MFA prompt.

## Troubleshooting

- If the connection drops, disconnect, switch gateway and reconnect.
- If MFA fails, check that your phone's date and time are correct.
- Still stuck? Raise a ticket in the IT portal with a screenshot of the error.

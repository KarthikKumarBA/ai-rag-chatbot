## Spotting Phishing

Be suspicious of messages that create urgency, ask for credentials or payment details, or come from look-alike domains.

## How to Report

1. Do not click links or open attachments in the suspicious message.
2. Use the **Report Phishing** button in your email client, or forward the email to security@northwind.example.
3. Post a short note in the **#security** channel if the message targeted several people.
4. Report within **1 hour** of noticing it.

## If You Clicked

Change your password from a trusted device, tell the security team in #security immediately, and do not wipe your laptop until security has reviewed it.
